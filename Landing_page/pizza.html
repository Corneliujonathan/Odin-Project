Pizza Recipe

Here is how to make a delicious pizza!


Description:

Pizza is a baked flatbread topped with sauce, cheese, and assorted ingredients, known for its crisp crust and rich, savory flavor. It combines a soft interior with a lightly charred exterior, making it one of the most popular comfort foods worldwide.

Ingredients:
  • Pizza dough (flour, yeast, water, salt, oil)
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Toppings (capsicum, onions, mushrooms, pepperoni, etc.)
  • Seasoning (oregano, chili flakes)

Step-by-step Guide:

  1. Prepare the dough: Mix flour, yeast, salt, water and Oil; knead until smooth and let it rise.
  2. Shape the base: Roll the dought into a round, thin crust.
  3. Add sauce: Spread tomato sauce evenly over ther surface.
  4. Add cheese and toppings: Sprinkle mozzarella and arrange your preferred toppings.
  5. Bake: Cook in a preheated oven at until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  6. Serve: Add oregano and chili flakes and slice while hot.
