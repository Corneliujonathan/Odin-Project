Salad Recipe

Here is how to make a delicious salad!


Description:

A basic fresh salad uses vegetables like cucumber, tomato, onion, and lettuce, tossed with a lemon-olive oil dressing and seasoned lightly with salt and pepper.

Ingredients:

  • 1 Cucumber
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 Tablespoons Olive Oil
  • 1 Tablespoon Lemon Juice
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

Step-by-step Guide:

  1. Prepare the vegetables
    • Wash and dry the cucumber, tomatoes, onion, and carrot.
    • Cut the cucumber and tomatoes into slices.
    • thinly slice the onion and carrot.
  2. Make the dressing
    • In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Combine and serve
    • In a large bowl, combine the prepared vegetables.
    • Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss to coat.
    • Serve immediately or chill before serving.
