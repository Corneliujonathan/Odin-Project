Pasta Recipe

Here is how to make a delicious pasta!


Description:

Fresh pasta uses flour, eggs, salt, and a little water, mixed into a dough, kneaded, rested, rolled thin, and cut into shapes before boiling.

Ingredients:

  • 2 Cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 Large eggs
  • 1/2 Teaspoon salt
  • 1-2 Tablespoons water
  • 1 Tablespoon olive oil

Step-by-step Guide:

  1. Make the dough
    • Place flour on a clean surface or in a bowl.
    • Create a well in the center and add eggs (or water for eggless), salt, and oil.
    • Slowly mix the flour into the wet ingredients until a rough dough forms.
  2. Knead the dought
    • Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
    • If sticky, add a little flour; if dry, add a few drops of water.
  3. Rest the dough
    • Cover with a cloth or wrap and let it rest for 30 minutes.
    • Resting relaxes the gluten, making rolling easier.
  4. Roll the dough
    • Divide into 2–4 pieces.
    • Roll each piece thin using a rolling pin or pasta machine.
    • Aim for 1–2 mm thickness.
  5. Cut the shapes
    • For fettuccine: cut long strips.
    • For lasagna: cut into rectangles.
    • For tagliatelle: cut slightly wider strips.
    • Dust with flour to prevent sticking.
  6. Cook the pasta
    • Boil water in a large pot with salt.
    • Add pasta and cook for 2–4 minutes until al dente.
    • Drain and serve with your favorite sauce.
